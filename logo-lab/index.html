Round 4 is your reference. Gold R on a sunburst, purple B holding mountains, the name underneath. Drawn as flat vector rather than as a rendered picture, because that is what a working logo has to be.

One thing I have to flag before this goes further. If that reference image came out of an AI image generator, do not adopt it as the logo. Two practical reasons. In the United States a work generated entirely by a machine, with no human authorship, cannot be copyrighted, so the logo would not be protectable. And the mountain inside the B looks like a photograph, which if it is one belongs to somebody. Tell me where the image came from and I will tell you whether it is safe to build on. Either way, everything below was drawn from scratch.
And one question I need you to settle, because your reference and your instruction disagree. Your instruction was: the mountains stay inside the B, in its holes, and do not go past it. Your reference picture has the range running through the B and continuing out to the right of it. I have built both, 4A and 4B. Tell me which one you meant and I will drop the other.

One naming question this raises. The reference writes the name as RISE & BALANCE. The legal entity and the signed-off copy both say Rise and Balance. An ampersand in a logo is a design device, not a name change, and I would keep it out of indexable text and out of the schema. But it is a deliberate choice rather than an accident, and it is yours and Sandra's.
