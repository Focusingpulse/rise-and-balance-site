The problem

You take a day off and spend it on your phone.

You go away and you are still the one they call. Somebody leaves and you quietly absorb the job without deciding to. You have a business, and the business has you.

Here is what most people get told about this: work on your mindset, get more disciplined, learn to delegate, find your why.

None of that is wrong. None of it is the problem either.

The problem is structural, and it has a shape you can see once somebody actually looks.

What we do

We do not start with what your business should look like. We start with where it actually breaks.

Over about two weeks we trace where the business depends on you personally:

  • Your inbox, and how much of it needs you specifically
  • Every interruption, and whether anyone else could have handled it
  • What needs your yes before it can move
  • What only you know
  • What happens when something goes wrong, and who gets called
  • Which customers and vendors will only deal with you

You end up holding one document: a ranked list of every place the business stops when you do, with a number at the top.

Here is what the top of that list looks like:

You have 43 points where this business depends on you. Nine of them stop everything the moment you are unreachable.

Your numbers will be yours. Most owners have never seen theirs written down, and almost all of them find something on the list they did not know was on them.

What we promise

  1. We will map, in writing, every place your business depends on you. You see it on paper before we change anything.
  2. We will build and install at least one working system before we finish. Not a plan for one.
  3. We will document the handoff, so it keeps working after we are gone.
  4. We will measure before and after on the things we changed, and show you both numbers.
  5. We will tell you which parts we are not touching, and why.

What we will not promise

This part is the reason to trust the rest of it.

Almost nobody puts this on a website.

  1. We do not promise revenue. We build the system that lets you run the business. The market decides the rest. If someone promises you growth, ask what they charge you when it does not arrive.
  2. We do not promise the business runs without you. Some things should stay with the owner, because the owner is the only one who should be doing them. We will tell you which ones.
  3. We do not promise balance as a feeling. It is not a mindset we install. It is what is left over.
  4. We do not promise speed. A system installed in a weekend is one you will undo in a month.
  5. We will say no. If your problem is not a system problem, we will tell you and pass on the work.

Our own standard, published:

If we cannot name one system we installed, and you cannot point at it running, we failed. We will say so in writing, and you should not pay for that part.
